## Phase Goal

**As an** operator, **I want to** see pipeline health and have failure and scale behavior proven by tests and a reproducible benchmark report, **so that** reliability and performance are demonstrated rather than claimed.

<objective>
Second metrics slice: both storage writers expose truthful position-based consumer lag, ClickHouse write latency and DLQ counts on their ops `/metrics`. Lag is computed from the worker's processed position against broker end offsets polled with kadm. The polling runs independently of the fetch loop, so lag keeps rising during a ClickHouse outage instead of freezing (RESEARCH Pattern 2). Stale series are deleted on revoke/lost (Pitfall 8).

Purpose: OBS-02 (consumer lag, CH write duration, DLQ). It is also the signal that the chaos (05-11), dashboards (05-13), System page (05-15/05-16) and bench recovery measurement (05-18) depend on.
Output: `kafkax.LagTracker`, `telemetry.ConsumerMetrics`, writer instrumentation and wiring in cmd/event-writer and cmd/alert-writer.

**Same-module note:** 05-06 and 05-07 share wave 2 and touch or compile against the same module (go.mod in 05-06, backend/test against kafkax in 05-07). With `use_worktrees: false`, run 05-05 → 05-06 → 05-07 sequentially (05-08 is Python-only), or use separate worktrees.

For the writers, processed_next equals the committed next offset, because both commit right after effects succeed. The detector (pin ≠ processed) is handled separately in 05-09.
</objective>
